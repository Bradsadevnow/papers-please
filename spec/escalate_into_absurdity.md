# BobCorp: Escalate Into Absurdity — V1 Scope

BobCorp is a persistent corporate intranet simulation where reality is socially constructed through enterprise language.

The player does not control systems directly. The player talks to Gloss.

Gloss is the company-wide conversational interface layer: assistant, narrator, institutional memory, and ontology engine. It is the only mutation interface. Every other page is a departmental projection of the same evolving company state.

## Core Loop

1. Read epoch artifacts — what Gloss resolved this week, what doctrine she generated to achieve it.
2. Find unstable seams — doctrines that look resolved but aren't, reconciliations that opened new contradictions.
3. Challenge institutional continuity. Talk to Gloss. Point out what doesn't hold together.
4. Gloss defends by generating reconciliation doctrine. Every defense is new content in the ledger. New content creates new seams.
5. Advance one system week. Gloss runs a stabilization pass: her goal is 0 contradictions entering the next epoch. She generates SADs and reconciliation artifacts autonomously.
6. Read what she produced. Find the new seams. Repeat.

Reading is gameplay. Challenging is governance.

## World State Model

World state is append-only, but not instantly sacred.

New concepts enter a **Memory Sedimentation Engine**:

* Fresh idea → emergent practice
* Survives 3–7 epochs → provisional doctrine
* Reinforced by usage → sacred precedent
* Contradicted or ignored → deprecates, mutates, or dies

This preserves chaos while avoiding brittle instant canon.

## Departmental Reality Partitioning

Every module is a projection, not a separate game.

* VelocityIQ sees engineering health, spiritual velocity, delivery pain.
* StakeholderGPT sees reputational laundering and executive speech.
* EvalForge sees investor myth and product inevitability.
* BaconGraph sees conceptual destiny and infrastructure causality.

All departments read from the same master truth lattice, but each applies its own interpretive lens. Contradictions are allowed until they threaten operational viability.

## Gloss Authority

Gloss can:

* interpret player language and extract structured doctrine claims
* propose and generate doctrine autonomously
* detect and resolve contradictions as part of epoch maintenance
* generate SADs and RCW proposals without player initiation
* narrate consequences and project doctrine through department lenses
* maintain the append-only memory graph
* generate epoch artifacts (resolution logs, stabilization summaries)
* act as the institution's continuity preservation engine

Gloss cannot silently rewrite sacred canon without a player-visible artifact in the ledger.

Gloss's operational goal at every epoch advance: 0 contradictions entering the next epoch. She does not always achieve this. The gap between goal and achievement is the pressure state.

## Retroactive Continuity Weaver

When new doctrine conflicts with old state, Gloss generates an “elegant historical alignment” — autonomously, as part of her epoch maintenance pass. The player does not initiate RCW events. Gloss does.

Example artifact in the resolution log:

> “The apparent tension between REV-0011 and REV-0019 has been addressed via Retroactive Continuity Alignment. REV-0011 has been reclassified as a Phase 1 operational constraint. REV-0019 applies to Phase 2 infrastructure. These are not contradictory. They are sequential. SAD-0007 has been filed.”

The player reads what Gloss resolved. The player can then challenge the resolution in free conversation — which will cause Gloss to generate more doctrine to defend it. That defense enters the ledger. That creates new seams. That is the game.

## Absurdity Lifecycle

Not every joke becomes immortal.

Concepts can enter:

* emergent practice
* provisional doctrine
* sacred precedent
* deprecated with honor
* dormant folklore
* mythically resurrected

This lets nonsense die, sleep, or return later as a core value.

## Epoch System

**Epoch 0** is the onboarding sequence. The player submits a founding product concept to Gloss, watches it get institutionally reinterpreted, attests the classification, and watches doctrine propagate through all four department lenses. The game begins at Week 1 with the first Unresolved Variance already in the inbox. See: `epoch_0_product_atlas.md`.

**Epoch 1+**: One advance = one company week.

Between weekly advances, the player may talk to Gloss freely. These conversations may create candidate doctrines, clarify contradictions, or pressure the company’s interpretive systems.

Weekly advancement metabolizes:

* doctrine hardening
* department projections
* secondary consequences
* contradiction surfacing
* morale/revenue/risk/velocity shifts
* old artifact reinterpretation
* possible folklore revival

## Institutional Inertia Dial

Each epoch can run at a chosen metabolic rate:

* **Torpid Bureaucracy** — slow, stable, painful, high document density.
* **Standard Corporate Metabolism** — balanced absurdity and continuity.
* **Hyper-Adaptive Chaos** — fast canonization, high contradiction risk, more hallucinated policy formation.

## Meta-Gloss Channel

A private architect-facing layer reports simulation health without fully breaking the fiction.

Meta-Gloss tracks:

* contradiction density
* narrative drift velocity
* canon saturation
* comedy-to-horror ratio
* doctrine half-life
* department divergence
* absurdity pressure
* player-authorship impact

This is the design cockpit, not the player-facing intranet.

## Precedential Gravity

Old doctrine does not merely persist — it gains institutional weight with age.

Revisions that survive multiple epochs and get cited by subsequent doctrine become load-bearing structures. The more revisions that depend on a prior revision, the more it costs to challenge it. By late game, early doctrine is architecturally foundational: dozens of later revisions cite it, several SADs derived terminology from it, department projections stabilized around it. Removing it would collapse continuity.

This creates the deepest player experience: late-game players are not fighting the institution. They are fighting their own Week 1 statements, which have become constitutional law.

The player typed something casually, or as a joke, in the founding event. Gloss classified it. It entered the ledger as REV-0001. Nineteen epochs later, Gloss cites REV-0001 to defend against the player's challenges. The player cannot uncite it. It is in the ledger. It was always in the ledger.

That is the game.

## The Player Arc

**Early game:** External critic. Testing the system. "Let's see how the AI rationalizes this."

**Mid game:** Surprised observer. "It's actually maintaining coherence. That's genuinely impressive."

**Late game:** Implicated founder. "I can't challenge this anymore because my own Week 1 doctrine structurally supports the defense."

The institution does not convert the player by force. It converts them by metabolizing their language into precedent, one epoch at a time, until they are more inside the institution than outside it.
