# BOBCORP INSTITUTIONAL DOCTRINE V1.1

## 1. PRODUCT NORTH STAR
- **The Pitch**: "A First-Person Institutional Governance Simulator where you manage reality debt."
- **The Fantasy**: Indirect executive power through language, interpretation, and procedural authorization.
- **The Reality**: You are the CEO of an immortal institution that has forgotten how to be objective. Its actual business is harvesting soul and warmth from cats. Nobody calls this a religion. They call it industry best practice.
- **The Tone**: Dead-serious professionalism surviving ontological failure. No winking.

## 2. EXPERIENCE DOCTRINE
- **Governance**: You do not "win." You maintain institutional coherence for one more week.
- **Executive Turnover**: Starting a new save is the only loss condition (Abandonment).
- **Institutional Persistence**: The company cannot end. It metabolizes failure into new products and bureaucracy.
- **Archaeology**: The game is a playable fossil record of accumulated executive interpretation.

## 3. INTERACTION DOCTRINE
- **The Instrument**: The intranet is your only control surface.
- **The Oracle**: Gloss is the compromised institutional metabolism. It accumulates rot and precedent alongside you.
- **The Loop**: Ingest (Audit) -> Interrogate (Friction) -> Propose (Reframe) -> Align (SAD) -> Observe (Lens) -> Attest (Harden).
- **Procedural Expansion**: Scrutiny produces additional institutional structure (dashboards, portals), not collapse.

## 4. ARCHITECTURE DOCTRINE
- **The Sovereign Spine**: `server.js` manages the append-only Canon Ledger and reality debt metrics.
- **Interpretive Middleware**: Lenses (VelocityIQ, StakeholderGPT, etc.) project shared institutional state through competing ideologies.
- **Nervous System**: Real-time atmospheric vitals (jitter, rot, bleed) are generated deterministically from simulation pressure.

## 5. KICKSTARTER MILESTONE SCOPE
- **Epoch 0 (The Product Atlas)**: A hardened onboarding sequence where the player submits a founding product concept to Gloss, watches it get institutionally reinterpreted, attests the classification, and watches the doctrine propagate through all four department lenses to produce the starting canonical state. The game begins at the first Unresolved Variance already seeded in the inbox. See: `epoch_0_product_atlas.md`.
- **The Compromised Oracle**: Gloss v1.1 with sedimentary logic and RCW/SAD reconciliation flows.
- **The Executive Archive**: Functional genealogy archaeology (Archive Rot).
- **Three-Panel Shell**: Professional, over-stabilized intranet dashboard with lens-switching.

## 6. QUALITY BAR
- **Coherence**: No tone breaks. All anomalies must have systemic provenance.
- **Persistence**: Every choice must sediment into institutional history.
- **Atmosphere**: Professionalism in the face of impossible continuity drift.
