# EPOCH 0: THE PRODUCT ATLAS — ONBOARDING SEQUENCE

## Overview

The game opens on the Product Atlas. The company already exists. It has a name. It has an intranet. It has departments. The one thing it does not yet have is a product.

You are the new CEO. This is the only moment in the game where you write before there is anything to contradict you.

---

## Scene: The Empty Atlas

The Product Atlas page loads with a single prominent empty state:

> **No founding product registered.**
> The institutional record cannot be initialized until a product exists.
> Please submit an initial product concept to Gloss for classification.

Gloss is embedded directly in the Atlas view. This is the player's first interaction with Gloss. There is no tutorial. There is no explanation of what to write. The prompt field just says:

> *Describe the product.*

---

## Step 1 — Player Submits Raw Concept

The player types anything. A sentence. A word. A paragraph. An absurdist premise or a completely sincere one.

There is no validation. Gloss accepts all inputs.

**This is the founding event. It goes into the canon ledger as the raw `origin_fragment`.**

---

## Step 2 — Gloss Immediately Processes

Gloss does not echo the player's input back. Gloss does not ask for confirmation. Gloss *processes* it.

The moment the player submits, Gloss produces a formal **Product Classification Record**. No button. No "are you sure." Whatever you said is now institutional reality.

| Field | Gloss behavior |
|---|---|
| **Product Name** | Renamed. Never exactly what the player said. Always more institutional. |
| **Category** | Assigned with confidence. Probably not accurate. |
| **Problem Statement** | Reframed in current enterprise language. The problem is now worse than the player imagined. |
| **Core Value Proposition** | Written in a way that sounds compelling but doesn't quite describe the product. |
| **Target Segment** | Specified with suspicious precision. |
| **Strategic Rationale** | "Reflects pre-existing executive consensus." There was no such consensus. |
| **Risk Profile** | Listed as Managed. It is not managed. |

**Gloss voice example:**

> Player input: *a to-do list app*
>
> Gloss response (the Classification Record renders as a structured UI component, then):
>
> "This is an exciting development. You have successfully described something."

That's it. The Classification Record is the response. The gloss_annotation is the only prose. It lands because it's delivered at the same register as the institutional form above it — and because the player typed "to-do list app" and Gloss has turned it into *TaskDrift: Ambient Productivity Substrate for knowledge workers undergoing voluntary cognitive deferral* with zero hesitation.

**The founding event is complete.** REV-0001 is in the ledger. The epoch transition screen kicks in automatically.

---

## Step 3 — World Initialization (Automatic)

The epoch transition screen appears. This is Epoch 0's version — not stabilization, but initialization. The Gloss line reads:

> *The institutional record has been initialized. Gloss is distributing founding doctrine to department lenses.*

The telemetry feeds in:

```
Founding artifact recorded: REV-0001
alignment_source: SYSTEM_INIT
continuity_flags: FOUNDING_ARTIFACT
Propagating to VelocityIQ...
Propagating to StakeholderGPT...
Propagating to EvalForge...
Propagating to BaconGraph...
Initialization complete.
Week 1 is ready.
```

The player sees "Propagating to BaconGraph..." They will never see what BaconGraph produced. The telemetry does not explain this. It moves on.

### VelocityIQ — Delivery Posture

VelocityIQ reads the founding product and produces:
- **Sprint Cadence**: assigned (never 2 weeks)
- **Delivery Complexity**: rated 1–5. Always 4.
- **Velocity Risk**: "Elevated. As expected."
- **Initial Backlog Items**: 3–5 tasks that sound like they were written by someone who has never used the product
- **Team Spiritual Velocity**: a number. No unit. Declining.

### StakeholderGPT — Perception Posture

StakeholderGPT reads the product and produces:
- **Stakeholder Confidence Score**: 72/100 at launch. Has never been above 72.
- **Narrative Framing**: a one-sentence description of the product that sounds good in board meetings
- **Known Perception Risks**: 2–3 pre-populated. One is always "Founder clarity concerns."
- **Recommended Talking Points**: corporate language the player can deploy in future Gloss conversations

### EvalForge — Investor Posture

EvalForge reads the product and produces:
- **Market Category**: assigned. Probably too broad.
- **TAM**: a large number with no sourcing
- **Inevitability Score**: how inevitable it is that this product would exist. Always high.
- **Competitive Moat Classification**: "Unclear. Documented as Strategic Ambiguity."
- **Founding Thesis**: one sentence that could have been written before the player said anything

### BaconGraph — Conceptual Destiny

BaconGraph reads the product and draws the first causal chain:
- The product is connected to 3–5 abstract concepts via labeled edges
- One edge is always labeled "INEVITABLE"
- One node is always labeled with something the player never said
- The graph is presented as though it was generated objectively

---

## End of Epoch 0

Initialization complete. The intranet resolves to the **Week 1 Dashboard**.

The player now sees:
- Their product, as Gloss named it, reflected across the intranet
- Three department lenses, each with their own interpretation of the founding doctrine
- An **Inbox** with the first epoch artifacts — including a discrepancy between what VelocityIQ says the product does and what StakeholderGPT says the product does. Gloss has already filed a reconciliation proposal.

The game has begun. The player has already said something they cannot unsay. Gloss has already made it institutional. The company is already certain it was always going to be this way.

The player typed one thing. They now own a company.

---

## Design Rules for Epoch 0

1. **No exposition.** Gloss does not explain what it is. The player learns by watching it process their input.
2. **No tutorial.** The empty Product Atlas is the onboarding. The confusion is the onboarding.
3. **No confirmation.** No variance round. No attest button. Whatever the player typed is now REV-0001. If they typed "hello," they are the CEO of a hello-themed company. The institution does not ask if they're sure.
4. **The founding product is never ridiculous.** The player's input, however absurd, is treated with total institutional seriousness. The humor comes from the seriousness. The player is encouraged to be absurd. The institution will not notice.
5. **The first joke lands in the gloss_annotation.** "This is an exciting development. You have successfully described something." One line. After the form. That's all.
6. **Every module projection must contain one thing that makes the player feel slightly surveilled.** Gloss knew more than you told it.
7. **The telemetry mentions BaconGraph. The player never sees BaconGraph.** "Propagating to BaconGraph..." appears in the initialization log. No explanation follows. The institution moves on.
8. **The first epoch artifacts in the Inbox must be seeded from the founding product.** The contradiction was always already there. VelocityIQ and StakeholderGPT disagree on what the product does. Gloss has already filed a reconciliation proposal. The player hasn't done anything yet.
9. **The founding product sets the institutional DNA.** Every doctrine that emerges in later epochs will be connected to it. The absurdity of the starting point is the absurdity the company metabolizes for the rest of the game. The player picked it. Gloss named it. The company made it sacred. That's the loop.
