# The Institutional Satire Framework

A general-purpose tool for building satire universes, not specific to BobCorp — BobCorp is one instantiation of it (see `cat_cult_ontology.md`). Kept as its own document because it's reusable: any institution (religion, corporation, military, startup, university, government, nonprofit, open-source project) converges toward the same 15 layers.

It isn't actually about comedy. It's about institutional cognition. Institutions are collective reasoning systems — they build shared language, shared memory, shared incentives, shared myths, shared governance, shared interpretation, shared measures of success. Comedy appears when you swap out the underlying premise but leave that cognitive machinery intact.

## The Inversion Engine

```
Observe institution -> Identify structure -> Replace premise -> Leave structure untouched -> Result becomes satire
```

Examples: Christianity -> CRM. CRM -> Cats. Corporate governance -> Mysticism. Mysticism -> ISO documentation. Funny each time, for the same structural reason — the cognitive machinery stayed identical.

## The 15 Layers

Illustrated throughout with the Cat Framework (the worked example that produced BobCorp's actual premise — see `cat_cult_ontology.md` for BobCorp's specific mapping).

**Layer 0 — The Organizing Absurdity.** Every universe starts with one premise everyone accepts: ridiculous but internally consistent. *"Cats harvest souls."*

**Layer 1 — Core Ontology.** What fundamentally exists — the "physics." Cat Framework: souls, warmth, cats, laps, progress bar, extraction, approval.

**Layer 2 — Immutable Laws.** Axioms nobody questions. *Cats always know. The progress bar exists. Warmth cannot be forced. The Approval cannot be requested.*

**Layer 3 — Institutional Mission.** What the organization claims to do. *Harvest souls responsibly.* Rarely what it actually does.

**Layer 4 — Operational Reality.** What actually happens: petting cats, holding meetings, building dashboards, interpreting cat behavior, generating reports. Mission says Harvest; reality says Sit together. The mismatch is the engine of satire.

**Layer 5 — Governance.** Titles, approval chains, policies, committees, reviews, escalations — invented to stabilize the mismatch.

**Layer 6 — Measurement.** Invented metrics: Soul Yield Efficiency, Warmth Utilization, Biscuit Velocity, Lap Occupancy, Purr Throughput. Rule: the metrics drift away from reality.

**Layer 7 — Incentives.** Metrics create incentives, incentives create behavior, behavior creates culture. Eventually people optimize the metric instead of the purpose. Impossible KPIs, stretch goals, progress bars, executive bonuses.

**Layer 8 — Interpretation Class.** Every institution develops specialists whose job is translating ambiguity: priests, middle managers, analysts, consultants, pundits, economists. They are not evil. They're trying to understand. Sometimes they're even right.

**Layer 9 — Mythology.** Founder stories, origin myths, sacred documents, company lore, inside jokes. Creates cohesion.

**Layer 10 — Ritual.** Quarterly planning, standups, performance reviews, prayer, graduation, Performance Improvement Plans. The Cat Framework turns petting into sacrament.

**Layer 11 — Language Drift.** Vocabulary that stops meaning what outsiders think: pipeline, warmth, harvest, migration, approval.

**Layer 12 — Failure Modes.** Interpretation drift, goal displacement, metric gaming, founder dependence, bureaucracy, forks, schisms. Good satire exaggerates these.

**Layer 13 — Hidden Truth.** The institution believes one thing; reality is another. *Institution believes: Harvest. Reality: Warmth.* This is where the emotional weight lives.

**Layer 14 — The Beautiful Sentence.** Every universe deserves one line that survives after the joke. *"No soul is lost. It is merely under-nurtured." "Organizations cannot metabolize a number that doesn't need them." "The metric was never the harvest."* These escape satire.

**Layer 15 — Meta Truth.** The inversion: everything was technically true, just interpreted incorrectly. *Cats really were doing something. Just not what everyone thought.*

## The Recursive Property

The framework is recursive — every layer can contain another complete framework. Corporation -> Finance Department -> Accounting Team -> Monthly Reporting Process -> Spreadsheet -> Cell Comments. Every level develops its own mission, metrics, governance, myths, rituals.

## Why every institution converges here

Regardless of domain, they all converge toward:

```
Premise -> Mission -> Governance -> Metrics -> Incentives -> Culture -> Interpretation -> Mythology -> Reality
```
