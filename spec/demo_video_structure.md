# DEMO VIDEO STRUCTURE — CONSTITUTIONAL DOCUMENT

## Format

Not a trailer. Not a hype reel. Not rapid cuts.

The video is a **disturbingly plausible enterprise software walkthrough.**

It should feel like leaked internal onboarding footage. Like a SaaS product demo someone recorded without permission. Like an executive orientation session that went somewhere wrong at a pace that was hard to pinpoint.

The audience's job is gradual recognition:

> "wait… this company is impossible."

The recognition is delayed. The delay is the mechanism. Do not accelerate it with music, editing, or voiceover signaling. Play it straight. Trust the institution.

**Total target runtime:** 4–6 minutes. Long enough for the epoch advance to land. Short enough that the escalation feels compressed, not slow.

---

## Marketing Doctrine

Do NOT present this as:

- a crazy AI comedy game
- unhinged procedural satire
- lol absurdism
- "what if a company went off the rails"

Present it as:

> a real enterprise simulation product.

The audience discovering the absurdity themselves is what makes it hit. If the marketing tells them it's funny, they wait for the joke instead of inhabiting the institution. The joke only works if they've stopped expecting it.

**The tagline register:** institutional. Dry. Professional. Could be on a real SaaS landing page. The absurdity of the product description is not flagged. It is presented as a feature.

### 3. Week 1 — The Loop (3:00 – 4:30)

Dashboard resolves to Week 1. Inbox visible. One Unresolved Variance loaded:

Player clicks the Friction Spark. Variance pipes into Gloss.

Player proposes a reframe to Gloss. Something that sounds reasonable — maybe just an observation that the cat sits on the keyboard sometimes, nothing more. Gloss accepts it, reframes it institutionally, and proposes a candidate doctrine.

> Candidate doctrine: **Keyboard Access Policy v17.4.** The Cat's occupancy of designated input hardware constitutes an approved override event, requiring no further authorization.

Player sits with this for a moment. Attests.

> Doctrine hardened. Revision #0002 entered. The institution has updated its relationship to Approval.

**What the audience understands:** They just watched the single most sacred concept in this world — Approval, which by institutional law cannot be requested — get satisfied retroactively by a cat sitting down. The institution accepted it instantly. This is how the company works.

## What This Video Does Not Include

- Voiceover explaining the game
- Music that signals comedy or horror
- Rapid editing
- The developer's face or voice
- Text captions summarizing what just happened
- Anything that tells the audience how to feel

The audience is inside the institution for 5 minutes. The institution does not explain itself. Neither does the video.

---

## The One Line That Must Land

> "One variance resolved. One variance generated. This is the expected ratio."

If that line lands, the video works.

If the audience reads that line and feels the specific horror of a system that is optimized to perpetuate itself rather than resolve anything — and simultaneously recognizes that it is also hilarious — the game is sold.

Everything else in the video is setup for that line.
