# Founding Frame

This is the canonical frame for the game.

The Battleshits transcripts are the company's seed corpus and founding
documents. They establish how the institution thinks: every objection becomes
product feedback, every contradiction earns another round of elaboration, and
the final decision follows incentives rather than truth.

JARDOS is the company's internal AI and institutional immune system. JARDOS is
not the company and is not the main subject of the archive. The documents are
about the company: its products, claims, decisions, tests, finances, incidents,
arguments, and consequences.

JARDOS originally stood for **Joint Administrative Resolution Documentation
Operating System**. Current employees rarely expand the name and generally do
not remember that it is an acronym.

JARDOS tries to keep the company papered over. It does not erase evidence or
tell obvious lies. It reclassifies failures, narrows definitions, changes what
numbers mean, records objections without resolving them, divides contradictions
among departments, and creates new documents when old documents disagree.

Each document should sound reasonable by itself. Together, the archive should
reveal exactly what happened.

The player's job is to assemble the single accusation JARDOS prevented the
company from ever writing down.

The prose targets grades 10 through 12. The format may be specialized, but the language and
the central contradiction must be understandable without professional or
technical credentials.

The comedy engine comes from the founding corpus: a ridiculous company claim is
examined with sincere intelligence until its internal logic becomes funnier and
worse than a standalone joke. The people are intelligent. Their incentives make
them selectively unreasonable. JARDOS makes their incompatible accounts coexist
without allowing the archive to produce a conclusion.
