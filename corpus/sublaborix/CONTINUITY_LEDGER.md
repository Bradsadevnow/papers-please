## Launch pricing hypothesis

- **Enterprise Capacity subscription:** $49,500 per month.
- **Included capacity:** up to 2,500 completed Node-hours per month.
- **Target assignment time:** qualified Node assigned within 30 minutes for
  standard work submitted during coverage periods.
- **Target completion:** 95% of accepted standard tasks delivered by the client
  deadline.
- **Overage:** not included in the founding offer; work beyond 2,500 hours queues
  into the next coverage period unless separately approved.
- **Initial contract term:** 12 months after a 30-day design-partner pilot.

These are founding hypotheses in Artifact 001, not yet validated facts.

## Initial Node economics hypothesis

- Nodes are paid only for accepted task time recorded by the platform.
- Launch planning assumption: average direct cash compensation of $18.50 per
  completed Node-hour.
- Qualification, verification, payment, support, and rework are additional
  variable costs.
- Reserved availability is not compensated in the founding model.
- Platform credits may pay for advanced qualification reviews and priority
  access to future tasks; credits are not cash and are not transferable.

## Founding performance definitions

- **Assignment:** a task has been routed to a qualified Node who accepted it.
- **Reserved availability window:** a period a Node voluntarily marks available
  for routing.
- **Acceptance rate:** accepted assignments divided by assignments offered.
- **Fill rate:** client tasks assigned within the promised response window.
- **Completion rate:** accepted tasks delivered by their recorded deadline.
- **Reassignment:** an accepted task moved to another Node before completion.
- **Throttle:** a temporary reduction in task-routing priority.

## Facts that must not drift

- A Node agreement says Nodes choose their availability and may reject tasks.
- The enterprise offer promises predictable capacity rather than access to any
  named individual.
- Operations needs enough acceptance inside reserved windows to meet the
  30-minute assignment target.
- Full use of 2,500 hours creates materially worse margin than the base forecast.
- The performance ledger records offers, acceptances, refusals, timing,
  reassignment, output review, and throttling.
- Northstar's pilot must succeed for the company to have credible external
  validation.
