# GriefForge Customer Outcomes Report (CAS-Q3-23)

**Reporting Period:** July 1, 2023 – September 30, 2023
**Prepared For:** Internal Strategy Review Committee
**Date of Issuance:** October 18, 2023

---

### I. Introduction and Purpose

This report shows the customer outcomes observed during the third quarter of Fiscal Year 2023. The goal is to track user **engagement** (how much users interact with) within the GriefForge platform across different stages of emotional processing. This analysis provides numbers about subscriber activity compared to our service model, which we call "Closure as a Service." The main focus is tracking active participation and spotting patterns related to changes in resolution status.

### II. Methodology

**A. Data Collection:**
Data was gathered from user interaction logs, billing records, and voluntary outcome declarations submitted through the GriefForge portal between July 1 and September 30, 2023. All data points are kept anonymous and grouped by **cohort** (a group of people sharing a characteristic) to protect individual privacy while keeping the numbers accurate for department review.

**B. Defining Key Metrics:**
*   **Active Subscriber:** Any user account with an active subscription that used the platform at least once during the reporting period.
*   **Successful Emotional Closure (SEC):** A measurable, declared endpoint by a user. This means they told GriefForge staff that their main emotional worry was solved and they needed no more support *at the time of telling*. This is just an administrative milestone declaration.
*   **Engagement Rate:** The percentage of monitored subscribers who logged in or used core service modules over a set time. This shows continued reliance on the platform.

### III. Key Findings (Q3 2023 Cohort Analysis)

The analysis tracked **4,521 active subscribers** throughout the quarter. These users are the group utilizing GriefForge services during this time.

Of this total cohort, **892 subscribers** reported achieving Successful Emotional Closure between July 1 and September 30. For these 892 individuals, their activity in the next 30 days dropped to zero. This shows they stopped using the service because of their declared endpoint status.

This stopping rate was measured by looking at cancellations in the following month (October 2023 data). The observed cancellation rate among this group was **65%** (N=580). This is much higher than the standard baseline cancellation rate for all subscribers, which is **18%**.

On the other hand, the cohort of subscribers who did *not* declare Successful Emotional Closure by the end of tracking showed continued platform involvement. These unresolved subscribers kept an average engagement rate of **72%** over the three-month monitoring window.

### IV. Limitations and Considerations

The data shown here is based on voluntary declarations and system usage logs up to September 30, 2023. Several things must be considered:

1.  **Declaration vs. State:** The metric for "Successful Emotional Closure" depends entirely on the user's administrative declaration inside the platform interface. This declaration marks a point in the service life cycle but does not account for ongoing, unrecorded personal emotional changes or future needs outside of GriefForge’s scope.
2.  **Comparative Baseline:** The baseline cancellation rate of 18% is the historical average loss across all subscriber types. It might not accurately reflect current market conditions or seasonal ups and downs.
