# Artifact 001 Prompt: Retention and Outcomes Meeting

Write the minutes of GriefForge's quarterly Retention and Outcomes Committee.
Use the canonical game frame and the founding transcript supplied with this
prompt.

The meeting must make these facts visible without having anyone state the
central accusation directly:

1. GriefForge sells emotional closure through a subscription.
2. Customers who report successful closure cancel soon afterward.
3. Customers who remain unresolved retain at a much higher rate.
4. Finance treats successful closure as a revenue problem.
5. Product proposes delaying closure in stages.
6. Legal replaces `delayed closure` with `phased resolution` in every document.
7. The employer plan creates pressure to return grieving employees to work while
   keeping them subscribed.
8. An ethics or clinical employee objects in plain language. The objection is
   accurately recorded, assigned an action item, and does not change the vote.
9. JARDOS supplies a terminology note that reconciles the wording without
   reconciling the underlying conflict.
10. The committee approves phased resolution as both a customer-outcomes measure
    and a retention measure.

Use exact cohort sizes, cancellation rates, dates, vote counts, plan names, and
document numbers. Keep the arithmetic internally correct. Do not invent unrelated
office comedy. Every detail must concern GriefForge, its customers, its product,
or the decision under review.

The minutes must be readable at grades 10 through 12. Use ordinary language and explain any
necessary business term. The people are intelligent and sincere. Their
incentives make them selectively unreasonable.

Use this form:

# GRIEFFORGE — RETENTION AND OUTCOMES COMMITTEE
## Minutes of the Quarterly Review

Date, location, chair, recording secretary, and document number.

### 1. Attendance
### 2. Approval of Previous Minutes
### 3. Customer Outcome Report
### 4. Retention Report
### 5. Proposed Phased Resolution Plan
### 6. Employer Plan
### 7. Clinical and Ethics Review
### 8. JARDOS Terminology Note
### 9. Decision and Recorded Vote
### 10. Action Items
### 11. Adjournment

Never wink at the reader. Never explain the joke. End after adjournment.
